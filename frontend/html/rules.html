Rules & about

Kairos is a real-time, two-player chess board played over a live connection. Standard chess rules apply.

Objective

Checkmate your opponent's king — put it under attack in a way it cannot escape, be blocked, or be defended against.

How pieces move

  • Pawn — forward one square (two on its first move), captures diagonally.
  • Knight — moves in an L-shape, and can jump over other pieces.
  • Bishop — moves any number of squares diagonally.
  • Rook — moves any number of squares horizontally or vertically.
  • Queen — moves any number of squares in any direction.
  • King — moves one square in any direction.

About this project

Kairos is built with a Phaser-powered board on the frontend and an Express + Socket.io backend for real-time play between two connected players.
